# Third-party material

This educational project is based on the Apache-2.0 Tiny Tapeout SKY Verilog template. The original LICENSE is retained. The bundled `tt/` directory contains Tiny Tapeout support tools at the commit recorded in source-lock.json; its license and source notices are retained in that directory.

The layout and generated verification material use the open SKY130 PDK and the sky130_fd_sc_hd standard-cell library. Copyright 2020 SkyWater PDK Authors. The SKY130 PDK is distributed under the Apache License, Version 2.0; see the root LICENSE for the license text and https://github.com/google/skywater-pdk for upstream licensing and notices. PDK source distributions and their notices remain available through the pinned Ciel releases recorded in source-lock.json.

Open-source EDA tools retain their own licenses, including Yosys, OpenROAD, OpenSTA, Magic, Netgen, KLayout, Icarus Verilog, and Verilator. Tool binaries, Python environments, and complete PDK installations are not bundled in the project archive. Refer to the corresponding upstream projects and the pinned LibreLane image for their licenses.
